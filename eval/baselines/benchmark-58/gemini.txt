### STEP 1: AMBIGUITY & BOUNDARY ANALYSIS

#### 1. Numeric / Date / Currency Boundaries
- **AC2 Approval Thresholds (Total EUR):**
  - Direct Manager only: Total < €500.00 (Boundary test: €499.99)
  - Manager + Finance: Total €500.00 to €5000.00 (Boundary tests: €500.00, €5000.00)
  - Manager + Finance + Director: Total > €5000.00 (Boundary test: €5000.01)
- **AC4 Expense Date Window:**
  - Valid window: 0 to 90 days ago (Boundary tests: 0 days / today, 90 days ago)
  - Invalid window: > 90 days ago (Boundary test: 91 days ago) or future date (Boundary test: +1 day)
- **AC5 Receipt Mandatory Threshold:**
  - Receipt optional / not mandatory: Line amount < €25.00 (Boundary test: €24.99)
  - Receipt mandatory: Line amount ≥ €25.00 (Boundary test: €25.00)
- **AC8 Transition Comment Length:**
  - Valid comment length: ≥ 10 characters (Boundary test: 10 characters, e.g., `"1234567890"`)
  - Invalid comment length: < 10 characters (Boundary test: 9 characters, e.g., `"123456789"`)

#### 2. Explicit & Implicit Refusal Rules
- AC4: Refuse submission if any line item date is older than 90 days (or in the future).
- AC5: Refuse submission if any line item ≥ €25 has no receipt attached.
- AC3: Refuse self-approval by the submitter.
- AC7: Refuse edits or re-submission attempts on a `rejected` report (terminal state).
- AC8: Refuse `rejected` or `changes-requested` transition if comment is missing or < 10 characters.
- Implicit: Refuse state transitions not allowed by the state machine (e.g., `draft` straight to `approved`).

#### 3. Ambiguities & Undefined Interactions
- **AC3 Submitter is Director (Top Level):** When a Director submits a report > €5000, who acts as the final approver? 
  - *Stated Default:* Reports submitted by a Director skip the direct manager step and require Finance + VP/C-Level approval.
- **AC4 Future Dates:** AC4 specifies "within the last 90 days", but does not explicitly state if future dates are permitted.
  - *Stated Default:* Future-dated expenses (e.g., expense date > today) are blocked at submission.
- **AC6 Non-EUR Currency Exchange Rates:** The AC does not specify how weekend/non-trading day exchange rates or missing conversion service responses are handled.
  - *Stated Default:* Convert using the last published official exchange rate for that date.
- **AC1 Editing in `submitted` state:** AC1 mentions editing in `draft` and `changes-requested`, but not whether editing is blocked during `submitted`.
  - *Stated Default:* Reports in `submitted` state are read-only and cannot be edited.
