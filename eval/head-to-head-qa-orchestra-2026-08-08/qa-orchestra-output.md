### Risks and Gaps

- **AC-2 band edges are stated inclusively/exclusively in an ambiguous way** ("under €500", "€500–€5000", "above €5000"). TS-017 to TS-020 assert the reading that €500 and €5000 both fall in the two-approval band; if the product intends otherwise these four scenarios must be re-baselined.
- **AC-3 "skips straight to the next level up" is ambiguous** for a manager whose own report also needs finance and director. TS-032 encodes the `[ASSUMPTION]` that only the submitter's own level is removed. The alternative reading (the report jumps directly to the final required level, skipping finance) is untested and needs product confirmation.
- **AC-5 does not say whether the €25 receipt threshold applies to the original currency amount or the converted EUR amount.** No scenario covers a `30.00 USD` line converting to `27.60 EUR` versus a `26.00 USD` line converting to `23.92 EUR` — clarify before automating.
- **AC-6 gives no rate source.** Rate values used in TS-007, TS-030 and TS-031 are illustrative; expected converted totals cannot be asserted exactly until the rate table or provider is fixed, and rounding rules (half-up vs. banker's, 2 decimals) are unspecified.
- **AC-1 does not state whether a `changes-requested` report resets prior approvals.** TS-033 assumes re-submission restarts the chain from the beginning; not confirmed.
- **AC-8 does not define the timestamp timezone**, so audit-trail assertions are limited to "a timestamp is present"; timezone-edge behaviour (a submission at 23:59 local crossing a UTC day boundary, and the 90-day window computed across that boundary) cannot be asserted reliably.
- The in-memory store means concurrency behaviour (TS-026) may differ from a production persistence layer; treat the result as indicative only.
- **Exploratory testing recommended** on: multi-line reports mixing in-window and out-of-window dates, mixed-currency reports straddling a threshold, receipt upload of oversized or non-PDF files, and the behaviour of the approval queues after `POST /api/reset` while a session is open.
