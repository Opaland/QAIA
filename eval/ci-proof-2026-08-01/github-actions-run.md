  without manual rework on a real **pilot** application — is untouched and remains unmeasured.
  A public demo app is explicitly not evidence for it (D-T17).

  the live app. It says nothing about whether those were the right scenarios to write.
- The green result depends on `saucedemo.com`, a third party. A future red run may be their
  outage; read the log before concluding regression.
