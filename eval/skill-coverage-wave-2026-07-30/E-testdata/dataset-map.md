## Assumptions

- **ASM-1** (product identity vs. the real target catalog) — dataset-generate step 6 forbids reusing a real product/company name, but the system under test (DemoBlaze) serves a real catalog and AC1/AC4 are only exercisable against a real catalog product id. This fixture resolves the conflict in favour of step 6: every product here carries a synthetic title and `externalCatalogId: null`. Any test that must hit the live target has to resolve a catalog id at runtime (e.g. from the first tile on the landing page) and must NOT read one from this file. This is a fixture convention, not a claim about the target's catalog.

- **ASM-2** (the 360 / 790 price pair) — The two prices used by the AC4-C2 accumulation case are taken verbatim from the already-validated test book (testbooks/cart-checkout.feature line 45: 'fetched prices of $360 and $790'), not invented here. They are attached to synthetic product titles per ASM-1. Numeric prices are carried over as-is; only names are synthesised.

- **ASM-3** (the exact whitespace used for AC7-C3) — 03-design AC7-C3 says 'a whitespace-only string (e.g. a single space)'. The source is silent on tab / NBSP / multiple spaces. This fixture uses exactly one U+0020 in OF-004 and does not assert anything about other whitespace forms.

- **ASM-4** (credit card values) — 03-design section 3b confirms the target performs no format/Luhn/expiry validation on the card field; oracle-generate was deliberately not invoked. Card values here are therefore intentionally non-numeric, obviously-synthetic strings ('SYNTHETIC-CARD-nnnn'). They are NOT test PANs and must never be treated as card-format fixtures.
