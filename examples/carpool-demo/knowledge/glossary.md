# Glossary — RideShare

- **Trip** — a single published departure (or one occurrence of a recurring trip): a route, a
  departure time, and a seat count offered by a driver.
- **Cost-share** — the amount a passenger pays a driver for a seat; capped at actual trip cost
  split across occupied seats (BR-KB-301), never a profit margin.
- **Recurring trip** — a trip template published with a repeat pattern (e.g. weekdays); each
  calendar date is an independent occurrence for booking and seat-availability purposes
  (BR-KB-305).
- **Occurrence** — one dated instance of a recurring trip.
- **Trust score** — rolling average of a rider's last 20 completed-trip ratings; gates a driver's
  ability to publish new trips (BR-KB-306).
- **No-show** — a booked rider who did not check in at the pickup point within the grace window
  after scheduled departure (BR-KB-303).
- **Booking restriction** — a temporary block on publishing or booking new trips, triggered
  automatically by repeated no-shows (BR-KB-303) or applied manually by trust & safety.
